### Species Nucleotide Count Comparisons 
<mark>Currently Ongoing</mark> <mark>R</mark>

Counting and plotting the percentages of each nucleotide of differing species using a DNA FASTA file. Uses Chargaff's rules to justify counting two bases. Data can be from Ensembl.
  
  
**Author(s):**

### Multispectral Forest Analytics Web App
<mark>Currently Ongoing</mark> <Python> <mark>Statistical Trends</mark> <mark>Mapping</mark> 

Interactive web map that extracts Google Earth NIR and SWIR data to determine a forest's health level. Algorithms can calculate the forest water stress level and vegetation level and relay the predicted health of a selected region. Further algorithms may calculate possible future trends using the historical Google Earth data.
   
   
**Author(s):**
*Benjamin Panackal, Reese Martinez, Bruce Li, Pari Thakkar, & Ethan Jones*

### Genome Assembly Quality Evaluator
<mark>Currently Ongoing</mark> <mark>Genetics</mark> <mark>Genome Assembly</mark>

An interactive dashboard web app that allows input of standard FASTA files to be evaluated. Quality of assemblies are based on standard metrics such as contiguity metrics (N50, L50), evolutionary completeness (BUSCO scores), and base composition (GC content).

**Author(s):**
*Tian Qiu, Ethan Atkinson-Sherrill, Jiaxin Chen, Siddarth Verma, & Zeke Zhang*
